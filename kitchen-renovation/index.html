Can I remodel without changing the layout?

Yes. Keeping the layout reduces cost and construction time. We can replace cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring and lighting while plumbing and appliances stay where they are.

Do I need board approval in a co-op or condo?

For most work beyond a refresh, yes. We prepare the alteration agreement paperwork, certificates of insurance and work schedule your building asks for, and coordinate with the managing agent.

Can you work in a small or galley kitchen?

Yes. We renovate compact apartment and galley kitchens, using space-efficient cabinetry, more storage, better lighting and durable surfaces without unnecessary structural changes.

Can I change the layout?

Often, depending on plumbing, electrical, structure and building rules. We review the proposed changes and coordinate the licensed trades and any permits required.

Do you renovate kitchens in rental units?

Yes. We work with landlords and property managers on cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, painting and fixtures, planned around turnover dates and budget.

What do you need for an estimate?

The address, photos of the current kitchen, rough measurements and what you want to change. Drawings, material ideas or a budget range help too. We then schedule a walkthrough if needed.
