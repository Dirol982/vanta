05 / Questions

Asked before every estimate

Can I remodel without changing the layout?

Yes. Keeping the layout reduces cost and time. We replace cabinets, counters, backsplash, flooring and lighting while plumbing and appliances stay put.

Do I need board approval in a co-op or condo?

For most work beyond a refresh, yes. We prepare the alteration paperwork, COI and work schedule your building asks for and coordinate with the managing agent.

Can you work in a small or galley kitchen?

Yes. Compact and galley kitchens get space-efficient cabinetry, more storage and better lighting without unnecessary structural work.

Can I change the layout?

Often, depending on plumbing, electrical, structure and building rules. We review the change and coordinate licensed trades and permits.

Do you work on rental units?

Yes. We work with landlords and property managers, planning cabinets, counters, flooring, paint and fixtures around turnover dates.

What do you need for an estimate?

The address, photos of the kitchen, rough measurements and what you want changed. A budget range or material ideas help too.
